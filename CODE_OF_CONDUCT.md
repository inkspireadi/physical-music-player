# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming, useful place for contributors of every background and experience level.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention, and sustained personal attacks are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security-reporting channel. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as practical.
